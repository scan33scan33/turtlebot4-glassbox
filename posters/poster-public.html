Maker Faire DemoA Glass-Box Robot

Tell it what to do. Watch it think.

No joystick. No remote control. No cloud. Say the words — this robot ranks its library of preprogrammed behaviors, picks the best match, and does the whole job itself, showing its thinking live on screen as it works.

1 · See

The same kind of AI vision as a self-driving car: a neural net spots 80 kinds of objects, ~6× a second, and judges distance with two lenses.

2 · Plan

No map is given. It draws the room's floor plan from scratch with lidar, and the classic A* algorithm plots the safest route — 3 fresh plans a second.

3 · Move

It chases a sliding point ahead on its route — that's why the driving looks smooth, not robotic — and it reroutes the moment something blocks the way.

Push the ball to the wall

The crowd favorite: it hunts the ball down, lines up behind it, and shoves it home until it feels the ball pin — using nothing but its eyes.

Say:"push the ball to the wall"

Follow me

It locks onto the nearest person and tags along at a polite 1-meter distance — it will never drive into you. Works on dogs too.

Say:"follow me" · "follow the dog"

Explore the room

Set it loose: it finds the emptiest open space, drives there, and repeats — roaming the whole room without hitting a thing.

Say:"go explore"
